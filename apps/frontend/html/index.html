Waiting for approval

Drafts and proposed changes waiting for qa, and for qp the completed runs nobody released yet. You approve another person's work: the database refuses an approval when you changed the item since its last approval, and a release when you created, changed or executed the run. A draft or a proposal can be returned with a reason; a returned draft stays an editable draft. A run is released or left unreleased: there is no hold or refusal.

Approval — an attributable record, not an electronic signature (21 CFR 11.50/11.70/11.200 arrive with Section 7).
