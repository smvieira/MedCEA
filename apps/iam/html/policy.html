Who may do what on the platform: one row per resource group, one column per role. A letter is a level the role holds (a dashed one is limited by a constraint). Click a cell to see what it gives. Red: the policy's checker refuses the cell; amber: a recorded waiver allows it.

Levels

Every version of the access policy. A change is drafted by an administrator, edited cell by cell in the Matrix, submitted, and approved by a quality assurance person who is none of its authors. Nothing approved is applied until the host operator runs ./apps/iam/apply.sh N.
Pick a database object, API route, host, MQTT topic or tool (or a resource group) to see which roles reach it, at which level, and which services do.
The services' own identities: the database role and MQTT user each one logs in as, and the most its role may ever hold (its ceiling). They are part of the platform skeleton and change only through git.
The identities that can act outside the access policy, who uses them and what records their use. Using one is break-glass: the record of it is in the History tab.

Versions waiting for confirmation

A bootstrap or rollback version is applied at once, by the person who ran apply.sh. Another quality assurance person confirms afterwards that it is what was intended, or disputes it. A confirmation is an attributable record, not an electronic signature.
