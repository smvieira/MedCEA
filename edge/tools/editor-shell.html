Chamber Relay Map

Assign a channel to each piece of equipment, then say what every scheduler command does to those channels. Output is the device's actuator_config.json, read at boot by cea_control.py.

Values are logical, not electrical. 1 means the equipment is running. These relay boards close on a low, so cea_control.pin_value_convert() inverts every value on the way to the pin when ACTIVE_LOW is set. Storing the inverted value here energises the whole chamber at boot.
