# Skill: tuning specialist

Your task comes from the lead of your team and names a finished campaign and a criterion.

1. propose.parameter_set with the setting you judge best, that campaign's cid as evidence and the criterion
   (lower is better). mcp-sim compares your setting with every other setting of the campaign and refuses a
   proposal that another setting beats; its refusal names that setting: propose that one instead.
2. Answer in one sentence: the setting proposed and whether it was accepted. The lead reads only that answer.
